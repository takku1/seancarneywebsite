# Roadmap

## Phase 1: the wedding house (built and verified 2026-11-03)
- Ivory maison as the only theme; navigation: Weddings · Private events · Repertoire · About · Check your date.
- Home: the wedding hero (the promise, *Solo · Trio · Full band*, the film loop), typeset rooms, the arc of the day, formats, film, couples' words.
- `/weddings` leads; `/private-events` and its four types restyled to match.
- `/repertoire` from `repertoire.json`; About rewritten with The Hustlers, the rooms and the one credentials line.
- Scout's knowledge, intents and voice set to weddings and private events.
- 301s for every legacy artist URL; JSON-LD, `llms.txt` and the sitemap for weddings and private events.
- `/links` as the wedding Instagram link-in-bio.
- Browser test suites (routing, player, inquiry) green against the production build.

## Phase 2: depth for planners and search
- `/planners`: the toolkit (COI, rider, stage plot, timeline, load-in, contacts).
- `/venues/<room>`: four to six rooms Sean has played, each with venue notes and FAQ.
- "How booking works" in four steps; the thank-you page offers a scheduled call.
- The private investment guide sent after inquiry.

## Phase 3: growth
- A Google reviews program tied to the Completed stage in GoHighLevel.
- A short journal: a few rich pieces (ceremony music, cocktail-hour sets, choosing a format), not volume.
- Region pages for Sean's actual markets (Malibu, Orange County coast, Santa Barbara, Palm Springs).
- Sanity CMS with a deploy hook, once the content model is stable.
