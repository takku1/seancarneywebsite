Élan Artists is the main presentation/roster reference; the cream design and technical architecture remain. Details: `10-sce-build-brief.md`.

## Positioning
**seancarneymusic.com is a luxury live-music house for weddings, led by Sean Carney, that also takes a select number of private events.** One booking covers the whole day, from a solo guitar at the aisle to the full band at midnight.

- **Weddings first:** every page is written for couples planning a high-end wedding, and for the planners and venues who recommend them. Planners are the multiplier, so every page is one a planner can forward.
- **Private events, quietly:** corporate evenings, charity galas, private estates and destination events are served from one "Private events" section.
- **Repertoire, not records:** the music is the songs couples and guests love (classics, standards, modern favorites), arranged for the room. Sean's songwriting appears only as the custom song written for a couple (`/custom-song`), with his credits in that context; the site carries no artist-catalog, rock, country or western framing.
- **Market:** Los Angeles, Orange County, Santa Barbara and Palm Springs, plus destination weddings.

## The offer: one house, three formats
| Format | For | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** (voice and guitar) | ceremonies, cocktail hours, dinners, intimate estates | the most-booked format |
| **Duo** | cocktail hours, welcome dinners, intimate receptions | harmony and rhythm, still quiet enough to talk over |
| **Band** (The Scene) | receptions, dance floors, galas | the whole night |

The site sells the **arc** of the day rather than a single format: ceremony (solo) → cocktails (duo) → dinner (solo or duo) → first dance → reception (band). Couples book once and the music changes with the day.

## Proof
- **Rooms:** the venues Sean has played, typeset by name: Hotel del Coronado, The Beverly Hills Hotel, The Ritz-Carlton Laguna Niguel, Terranea, The Biltmore. Named rooms are the luxury-correct proof; they need no logo permissions.
- **Couples and planners:** named testimonials with the occasion and place, used only with consent.
- **Scale and craft:** more than 1,000 events across fifteen years (confirm the figure; see the launch checklist).
- **Credentials:** one restrained line on About: Sean's music has been heard in the Paris Olympics coverage, the NFL Playoffs and on Netflix.
- No marketplace badges (Thumbtack, The Bash) anywhere.

## Voice
A maître d', not a hype man: warm, brief, assured. Short declarative sentences, no exclamation marks, no urgency or scarcity claims, no slang. Prices are never stated in copy; proposals are tailored to the day.

## Pricing posture
Proposals are tailored to the date, the room, the format and the hours of music. A private investment guide goes to couples after they inquire. A public "from" price per format is an open decision for Sean (see `08-roadmap.md`).

## Measures of success
- North star: `inquiry_submit` per visit, then the share of qualified leads (`lead_tier` A and B).
- Supporting: "Check your date" clicks, wedding-film completions, scheduled calls after inquiry, planner and venue referrals (first-touch channel on each lead).
