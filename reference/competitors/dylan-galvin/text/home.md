## Frequently Asked Questions

Can you perform different music styles at one event?

Yes, Dylan can cover a wide range of styles, from classic hits to modern favorites. The setlist can be adjusted throughout the event to match the changing mood and audience.

Do you bring backup equipment in case of technical issues?

Yes, Dylan comes prepared with backup gear to ensure everything runs smoothly. This helps avoid interruptions and keeps the performance seamless.

Can you perform indoors and outdoors?

Yes, Dylan is fully equipped to perform in both indoor and outdoor settings. The setup is adaptable to different environments and venue requirements.

Do you offer custom song arrangements?

Yes, Dylan can create unique acoustic versions of songs to suit your event. This adds a personal and memorable touch to special moments.

What makes a solo performer a good choice for events?

A solo performer offers flexibility, easier setup, and a more intimate experience. It also allows for a smooth flow without the complexity of managing a full band.

Can you coordinate timing with other vendors?

Yes, Dylan works alongside planners, coordinators, and other vendors to ensure everything runs on schedule. This helps create a smooth and well-organized event experience.
