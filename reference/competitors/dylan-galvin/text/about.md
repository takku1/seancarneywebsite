### AWARDS

“Best Guitarist in the State of Maryland” — Phoenix Awards “WAM and IMEA” — Songwriting Competition Runner Up Berklee Annual Songwriting Competition Winner

Oscar Nuñez (The Office), Fabio, Dylan McDermott, Joseph DePinto (CEO of 7-Eleven), Bruce Springsteen’s producer Chuck Plotkin, Bedros Keuilian, and Charlene Tang. Audiences across Italy, Germany, Belgium, France and the U.S.

### COMPANY PERFORMANCES

The Smithsonian, Coca-Cola, Wells Fargo, LA Rams, Delta Airlines, Consumer Electronic Show, Tetrapak, Lincoln, CBRE, SAP, YPO and more.

### PREVIOUS VENUES

Waldorf Astoria, The Willard Intercontinental, The Inn at Perry Cabin, The Old Ebbitt Grill, The Venetian, M&T Bank Stadium, Northwest Stadium, The SLS Hotel, Montage, Ritz-Carlton and more.

### EDUCATION & MENTORSHIP

Berklee College of Music, Magna Cum Laude. Mentored by legends John Mayer, Paul Simon and Livingston Taylor (James Taylor's brother), plus John Mayer's own guitar teacher Tomo Fujita.

### ORIGINAL WORKS

Two independently released EPs, plus additional scoring for “Essential Church” — which reached #1 in the Apple TV documentary category.

### REPUTATION

5-star rated on every major booking platform — The Bash, GigSalad, Bark, Google and Thumbtack.
