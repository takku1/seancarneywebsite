# Meet Your Live Wedding Guitarist & Singer

Johnny Rockett is a premier destination guitarist and vocalist based in Charlotte, NC, specializing in wedding and corporate events. He creates a personalized live music experience for ceremonies, cocktail hours and corporate gatherings that complement each clients’ unique vision. With over a decade of experience captivating audiences, his versatile performances and ability to create unforgettable musical performances have made him the top choice for clients seeking elegance and energy in their entertainment.

Growing up in Louisiana in a musical family with seven siblings, Johnny was immersed in music from an early age. His mom played piano at a local church, while his dad sang and played guitar at family gatherings. Two of his sisters are full-time piano instructors, and another pursued a degree in voice, which further solidified music as a central part of his upbringing. Music was more than just a passion—it was and remains to be his primary method of connecting with others and understanding his place within his family, friend group and community. This early connection to music shaped his desire to perform and share what music has given him with others.

Whether it's an intimate gathering or a large corporate event, Johnny has the ability to bring his signature style to any setting, combining sophistication and high-energy performances that engage and inspire.

Having played at over 1,000 private events, Johnny is highly skilled at tailoring each performance to the specific needs of the audience. His talent has been featured at some of the most prestigious venues in the Carolinas, including The Biltmore Estate, VanLandingham Estate, Ballantyne Hotel, Aloft Hotels, Carmel Country Club, and The Ruth by Beau Monde. Johnny’s dynamic style resonates with diverse crowds, making him a sought-after performer for weddings, corporate functions, and private celebrations.

Johnny is also a prominent figure in the Charlotte songwriter scene. He co-founded a songwriter collective called "Keep It Original Events" in partnership with local songwriters. Songwriter rounds provide a unique listening experience where creatives share all-original music while connecting with their local community.
