- What is your process the day of the event?

#### What is your process the day of the event?

What is your process the day of the event?

My answer varies based on the type of event. For private parties or corporate events, clients can expect me to arrive 1.5 hours before the scheduled performance start time. For wedding events, I typically arrive at the venue three hours prior to the start of the event. This allows ample time for setup and sound check before the guests arrive.

- Do you play outdoor events too?

#### Do you play outdoor events too?

Do you play outdoor events too?

Yes, I do, as long as the weather is suitable. I provide a tent for outdoor events; however, if temperatures exceed 90 degrees or drop below 50 degrees, the event will need to be moved indoors to maintain a high standard of perfroamce and prevent damage to my equipment.

- How far in advance should I book you?

#### How far in advance should I book you?

How far in advance should I book you?

The timing for booking depends on the type of event and my availability. For weddings, I recommend couples reach out and finalize their booking anywhere from six months to a year in advance. For other event types, it’s ideal to inquire 1-3 months ahead of the event date to ensure the best chance of availability. Early booking helps secure your spot and allows ample time to tailor the performance to your needs.
