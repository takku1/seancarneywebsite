ABOUT

Johnny Rockett is a premier destination guitarist and vocalist based in Charlotte, NC specializing in weddings and corporate events. With over a decade of experience captivating audiences, his versatile performances and ability to create unforgettable musical experiences have made him the top choice for clients seeking both elegance and energy in their entertainment.

A seasoned “Top performer” on Thumbtack, and #1 Music vendor in North Carolina on The Bash, Johnny has partnered with multiple Fortune 500 companies, bringing his signature style to exclusive corporate gatherings. His extensive client portfolio features renowned brands like Aldi, Belk, Honeywell, Ford Performance, The University of South Carolina, Keffer Auto Group, NW Ravin and Clemson University.

Having played at well over 1,000 private events, Rockett knows how to tailor his performances to each unique audience, whether it's an intimate wedding ceremony or a corporate gala. His talent has been showcased at renowned venues such as The Biltmore Estate, VanLandingham Estate, Ballantyne Hotel, Aloft Hotels, Carmel Country Club and The Ruth by Beau Monde.

In 2024, he was the featured artist at “The Water Lantern Festival” in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, where he played for a breathtaking crowd of 10,000 attendees. Whether performing a heartfelt acoustic ballad for a wedding ceremony or energizing a corporate event with crowd-favorite hits, Rockett brings professionalism, passion, and unmatched musical expertise to every performance.
