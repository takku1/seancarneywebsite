## Ceremony

## ELEVATE YOUR WEDDING EXPERIENCE

## WITH WORLD CLASS ENTERTAINMENT

### Emotional | Captivating

I want your guests to arrive at your ceremony and audibly gasp at how perfect your wedding is. I'll personally walk down the aisle to greet them at the door with live music and transition seamlessly into your processional music.

01

PRO TIP: WALK SLOWLY! THIS IS YOUR MOMENT, BE SURE TO SAVOR EVERY SECOND OF IT.

## Cocktail Hour

### Dynamic | Engaging

High-energy entertainment is crucial at cocktail hour. I change performance styles here, adding percussive elements and syncopated bass lines in order to create a stunning performance that is sure to get your guests dancing and singing along.

02

PRO TIP: TRY TO MAKE IT TO THE LAST 15 MINUTES OF COCKTAIL HOUR. YOU DEFINITELY WON'T REGRET IT!

## First Dance

### Intimate | Romantic

Make your first dance even more special with an instrumental version of your favorite song, created just for you. I spend 30-40 hours on every song that I perform, meticulously creating a unique version that your guests have never heard before.

03

PRO TIP: SURPRISE YOUR PARTNER WITH AN ARRANGEMENT OF THEIR FAVORITE SONG.

## Reception

### Elegant | Sophisticated

It's time for your personal serenade. Up until this point, the entertainment has mostly been for your guests. Dinner music during the reception is just for you. I'll still walk table to table, performing for every guest, but I'll always make sure to come to your sweetheart table to give you and your partner a private performance.

04

PRO TIP: LET ME KNOW IN ADVANCE WHAT SONG YOU WANT TO HEAR, AND I'LL SAVE IT FOR THIS MOMENT.
