## MOSES LIN

Moses Lin is an award winning destination wedding guitarist that specializes in instrumental covers of popular songs. His unique style of music, engaging live performances and effortless sophistication have made him the most highly sought after live musician in Southern California.

Every song Moses performs is an instrumental version that he has created himself, simultaneously playing a song's melody, bass line, chords, and percussion without looping or backing tracks. His showmanship is further enhanced by his ability to have full conversations with wedding guests while performing these complicated arrangements from memory.

Featured on Netflix, CNBC, and virtually every major wedding publication, Moses is an industry leader, coaching wedding musicians in countries all around the world, and raising the standard for live music at luxury events.

With over a decade of experience as a full-time musician. Moses has performed over 1,000 weddings and events and his clients include Hollywood celebrities, Grammy-winning artists, Fortune 50 CEO's, Reality TV stars, Olympic gold-medalists, politicians, and many more.

## Musical Journey

It all started with a gold medal at a Sonatina Piano Festival in Chicago. I was six years old and fell in love with performing.

After high school, I enlisted in the United States Marine Corps and earned a spot in the esteemed Marine Corps Band. Only ten Marines in the world can hold the position of Marine Guitarist, and I became the resident guitarist at the Marine Corps Air Station Miramar. At the end of my enlistment, having earned the rank of Corporal, I moved to Orange County, where I began performing at venues like Disneyland.

Since then, I’ve grown to become the most highly sought after musician in Southern California, performing at VIP events, celebrity weddings and destination weddings in multiple states and countries.
