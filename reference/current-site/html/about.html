BIO

Sean Carney is a boundary-pushing singer, guitarist, and producer based in Los Angeles, known for his unmistakable sound that seamlessly blends rock, pop, and cinematic influences. His raw, unfiltered approach to music has earned him a dedicated following and made him a force on the global stage as a touring and recording artist. With his new album WANTED , Sean takes listeners on a sonic journey where rugged western vibes meet cinematic rock, showcasing his evolution as a genre-defying storyteller.

A Billboard-charting songwriter, Sean's music has resonated across a variety of media, with tracks featured on major platforms like Netflix, ESPN, Disney, and TNT. His work in the band WOLVES caught the attention of NBC’s Songland, where they were chosen to perform their original music on national television. Beyond the screen, Sean's songs have been featured in national advertising campaigns for brands like Weight Watchers and Hyundai.

In addition to his studio work, Sean has performed on some of Los Angeles' most iconic stages, including The Troubadour, The Roxy, The El Rey, and The Hotel Cafe. He's also had the honor of performing multiple times at The Crypto.com Arena for LA Kings hockey games, further solidifying his place in the LA music scene.

With a career that blends heartfelt lyrics, powerful performances, and genre-bending sounds, Sean Carney is an artist to watch as he continues to captivate audiences worldwide.
