Sean carney

MUSIC FOR OUTLAWS

Hailing from Los Angeles, CA, Sean Carney is a singer/songwriter who thrives off true human connection through music. From captivating live performances, to Billboarding Hot 100-charting singles, Sean's imprint on the world of music will be left for generations.

He has had his original music featured on The 2024 Paris Olympics, The 2025 NFL Playoffs, Hyundai Commercials, Netflix Movies such as "Meet Me Next Christmas" and many more.

Sean is a seasoned live-performer who has toured in over 50 countries. Sean offers a unique touch to any live event as a soloist or with his powerhouse band, The Hustlers.
